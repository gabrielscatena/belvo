# Frozen-action economic diagnostics

Accounting views use the same lender population and fixed monthly A commitment.
Each allocation view reconciles with its TOTAL row; only lender attribution changes.
Equal weights are primary. Pretest A-use weights are fixed across policies.
Own-policy A-use weights change with the policy and are sensitivity diagnostics only.

The tariff table subtracts nominal 1.0 per A attempt. It is a usage-priced diagnostic,
not the prepaid cash margin. Its TOTAL residual bridges back to the same cash margin:
usage-priced margin minus (fixed cost minus tariff charges). Under monthly accrual,
this residual may be negative and is not physical unused allowance.

The lambda table holds all actions fixed. Shadow-adjusted scores change while
accounting margins remain unchanged. No opportunity price is added to cash expenses.
No price is selected and no scarcity-aware policy has been reoptimized here.

These are April conditional replay diagnostics, not causal gains or a six-month
forecast. May-June admission, provider response and future balances remain unmodeled;
no contract projection is invented and no requirement forces prepaid units to zero.
Results inspected after April cannot be described as a new untouched test.
